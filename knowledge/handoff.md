# Handoff

The inbox for points handed over from other sessions, lanes or repositories, among them gams-www, that wait for integration or rejection. Each point carries `Received`, `Source`, `Target` and `Context`. Before a point is used, its source and current target are checked. Its durable content goes into the responsible document, the [journal](journal.md) records subject, source, target and result or reason for rejection, and the point is then removed here.
